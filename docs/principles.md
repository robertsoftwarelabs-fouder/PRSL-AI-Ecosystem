# PRSL-AI Ecosystem Principles

## Human-Centered Intelligence

Technology should enhance human capability, preserve autonomy, and create meaningful experiences centered around people.

## Privacy by Default

Privacy is a foundational principle. Systems should be designed with respect for personal data, transparency, and user trust.

## iRiel Intelligence

A new approach to intelligence focused on responsible, adaptive, and human-aligned interaction between technology and society.

## Living Ecosystem

The PRSL-AI Ecosystem is designed to evolve continuously through learning, collaboration, and responsible innovation.
